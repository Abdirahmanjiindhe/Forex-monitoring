Risk management

Everything I teach starts here — the entries matter less than this does.

Position size follows the stop, not the other way around. Calculate size from stop-loss distance and your max risk per trade — never the reverse.

A stop-loss is fixed once set. It's placed above supply or below demand and never moved mid-trade.

Risk per trade stays constant. No revenge sizing, no doubling up after a loss.

Targets come from structure. Exits are set at the last higher-high or lower-low, not an arbitrary R multiple.
